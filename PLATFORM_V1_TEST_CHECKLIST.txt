RBH SAFETY PLATFORM V1 — TEST CHECKLIST

DEPLOYMENT
1. Run supabase/PLATFORM_SITE_V1_METADATA.sql in Supabase.
2. Confirm the final validation returns:
   - RBH Insulation in public.organizations
   - admin_invites_table = organization_admin_invites
3. Upload the GitHub-ready ZIP to the repository.
4. Wait for the normal site deployment to complete.
5. Open /platform/ on the same deployed site.

PLATFORM ACCESS
1. Sign in with the Platform Owner account.
2. Confirm the Platform Site loads.
3. Confirm a non-platform account is denied if tested.

OVERVIEW
1. Confirm Companies = 1 before creating a test tenant.
2. Confirm RBH Insulation appears.
3. Confirm active user/report counts are reasonable.
4. Confirm recent platform feedback appears if feedback exists.

COMPANIES
1. Open Companies.
2. Confirm RBH Insulation appears.
3. Click RBH Insulation.
4. Confirm the company drawer opens.
5. Save a harmless metadata change, such as Platform Notes.
6. Refresh and confirm it persisted.

CREATE COMPANY
1. Create a clearly temporary company:
   Name: Platform Test Company
   Slug: platform-test-company
   Plan: Pilot
   Subscription: Trial
2. Optionally enter a fake initial admin email you control.
3. Confirm the company appears in Companies.
4. Open the company and verify the pending admin setup record appears.

FEEDBACK
1. From the RBH Company Site, submit a test item through My Profile > Support.
2. Return to /platform/.
3. Confirm the feedback appears under RBH Insulation.
4. Change its status to Reviewed.
5. Refresh and confirm the status persisted.

COMPANY SITE SEPARATION
1. Open /dashboard.html.
2. Confirm there is NO Platform navigation item.
3. Confirm RBH Safety Inbox, reports, Admin, profile, and workflow still operate normally.
4. Confirm the company site still shows RBH Insulation context.

CURRENT LIMITATIONS — EXPECTED IN V1
- Creating a pending Company Admin record does NOT yet create/invite a Supabase Auth user.
- Stripe is NOT connected; subscription data is manually managed metadata.
- The Employee role and final permission matrix are not yet implemented.
- Public report intake is still temporarily defaulted to RBH until tenant-specific reporting links are built.
- Do not onboard a real second customer until the separate-company login isolation test is completed.

NEXT SECURITY GATE
Build secure initial Company Admin invitation/account creation, create a fake Company #2 user,
then prove Company #2 cannot access RBH profiles, reports, notes, attachments, audit history, or feedback.
