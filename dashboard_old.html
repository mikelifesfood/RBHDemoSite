Adding or removing people. Accounts are managed in Supabase (they cannot be created from this page, for security). To add someone: Supabase → Authentication → Users → Add user, enter their email and a password, and check “Auto Confirm User.” To remove someone: delete them from that same Users list. A person appears here the first time they sign in.
Compliance note. This system logs and tracks hazards and near-misses in support of RBH’s Injury & Illness Prevention Program (8 CCR §3203). It is not the Cal/OSHA Form 300 log and not the serious-injury report. A serious injury, illness, or death — inpatient hospitalization (beyond observation or diagnostic testing), amputation, loss of an eye, serious permanent disfigurement, or death — must be reported to the nearest Cal/OSHA district office immediately, and no later than 8 hours (8 CCR §342). Cal/OSHA assistance line: 833-579-0927.
