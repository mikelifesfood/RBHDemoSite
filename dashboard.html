California safety & reporting notice. This system supports RBH’s Injury & Illness Prevention Program by documenting hazards, near misses, injuries, investigations, and corrective actions. It does not itself submit a required Cal/OSHA serious-event report or replace required injury-and-illness records. For a work-related death or a possible serious injury or illness, notify RBH management immediately. Under 8 CCR §342, a qualifying event must be reported to Cal/OSHA immediately — as soon as practically possible and ordinarily no later than 8 hours after the employer knows, or with diligent inquiry would have known, of the event. Section 342 contains a limited exigent-circumstances exception. Use the official Cal/OSHA reporting instructions for the current reporting method and district-office information.

Close — no fix needed

Use this when you have looked at the report and nothing needs to be fixed.

The report leaves the open lists but stays in history. Your reason and your name are saved with it.

Reopen report

Send a closed report back to Step 1 (Review). Its full history is kept.

Nothing is erased. Reopening starts the steps again at Review so new information is looked at before a new cause or fix is chosen.
  • The original report, notes, photos, Cal/OSHA answer and earlier fix photos are kept.
  • The earlier closing, your reason for reopening and earlier answers stay in the activity history.
  • The cause, fix plan, assignments, due dates, completion and approval are cleared for the new round.
  • Every closed report reopens at Step 1 (Review).

Start steps over

Send this report back to Step 1 without erasing its history.

This clears the work done so far. The cause, fix plan, type of fix, assignments, due dates, priority, completion and approval are reset. You cannot undo this.
  • The original report and photos stay the same.
  • The Cal/OSHA answer and the time RBH found out are kept.
  • Notes, activity history and earlier fix photos are kept.
  • A backup copy of the cleared work is saved.
